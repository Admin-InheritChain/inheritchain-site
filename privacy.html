// Privacy

What we collect.
What we never will.

Last updated: 2026 · InheritChain is in research + MVP design. This page covers the coming-soon site only.

01What we collect

Email address. If you join the waitlist, we store the email you submit. That's it — no name, no wallet, no identity verification.

Technical requests. This site loads fonts (Google Fonts) and scripts (jsDelivr) from third-party CDNs, which receive standard request data like your IP address, as any web request does.

02How we use it

Your email is used only to send product updates, research invitations and early-access notices for InheritChain. We don't sell it, share it, or use it for anything else.

Waitlist emails are stored in a private database (MongoDB Atlas). We also send ourselves a notification when someone joins.

03What we never ask for

We will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys or recovery credentials. Not on this site, not by email, not ever. Anyone who does is not us.

04Your choices

Want off the list, or want to know what we hold about you? Email contact@inheritchain.xyz and we'll delete or share your data on request.
