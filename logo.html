// Proposal 001 — Visual identity

The pin.

The cyan I is threaded through the C — one crossing over, one under: locked, not placed. Identity pinned inside the chain. The acid node is the asset held at the seam. Hard edges, no rounding, no gradients: the mark is built like the protocol — rigid, verifiable, permanent.
